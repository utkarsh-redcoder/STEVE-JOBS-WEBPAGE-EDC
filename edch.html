The people behind the products

Steve
Jobs

The man who changed how we interact with technology.

Steve Jobs was an American entrepreneur whose work helped bring personal computers, animated films and digital music into everyday life. As a co-founder of Apple, he shaped the company's direction through periods of remarkable growth and reinvention. His story also runs through NeXT and Pixar—and through the collaborators who turned ambitious ideas into products millions of people could use.

Chapter three · What endured

An influence,
built together.

Products are made by teams. Jobs's role was to help set direction, make choices, and tell a story people could see themselves in.

01

Technology for everyday life

Apple's teams helped bring personal computing, digital music, smartphones, and tablets to broad consumer audiences. Jobs played a central role in the company's product direction and launches.

02

A new kind of animation studio

As Pixar's investor and owner, Jobs supported the studio as it moved from computer graphics into feature animation. Pixar's artists, engineers, and filmmakers created the films.

Chapter four · Ideas to carry forward

Lessons in
the work.

A reading of the choices behind the story—not quotations, but useful ways to think about building.

01

Make room
for focus.

When everything is a priority, nothing is. The Apple story shows the value of narrowing attention and designing the experience around the person using it.

02

Begin again
with purpose.

Leaving Apple did not end the work. NeXT and Pixar became distinct chapters—and later, NeXT's technology helped make a return possible.

These takeaways are interpretations of Jobs's career, not direct quotations.
